# Death detection

This section decides **when FraseKill should appear**.

On most servers you can leave the adapter on **Auto** and FraseKill will try to detect the ambulance job you use.

You can also choose whether FraseKill should appear when the victim becomes incapacitated or when they are fully dead, depending on your medical resource.

If you use a supported ambulance script, you normally do not need to change anything else.

See **Medical compatibility** for the supported list.
