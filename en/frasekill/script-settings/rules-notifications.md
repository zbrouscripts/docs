# Rules and notifications

## Rules

You can choose whether players can open the rules from **View rules**.

Rules and blocked words are managed from the admin panel.

If a phrase breaks your server rules, FraseKill can stop it from being saved.

## Notifications

Enable or disable admin alerts and logs.

You can also change the name shown in Discord messages.

Webhook URLs are not shown in the panel and stay in:

```text
server/webhooks.lua
```
