# Details and related terms

ZBrou FraseKill is a **FiveM kill phrase script** for servers that want custom kill messages, kill text or configurable death messages. It may also be searched as **PhraseKill FiveM**, **FiveM kill phrase**, **custom kill message FiveM**, **death message FiveM**, **custom kill text**, **kill message script** or **FiveM PvP kill message**.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I show a custom phrase after killing a player in FiveM?
FraseKill lets each player configure their own presets and shows the selected message to the victim after the kill.

### Are FraseKill and PhraseKill the same idea?
Yes. “PhraseKill”, “kill phrase”, “Frase Kill” and “kill message” are commonly used for similar custom-message systems.

### Can it be sold as a VIP perk?
Yes. Access can be managed through the admin panel, ACE, jobs/groups or Tebex plans.

### Can players create different styles?
Yes. Each player has three complete presets with fonts, animations, glow, size, position and fixed or random mode.
